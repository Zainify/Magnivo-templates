IMMEDIATE PROPERTY PROTECTION

Emergency Isolation Checklist

Perform these 4 crucial technical isolation steps while our mobile unit is en route.

Step 01

Main Water Ball Valve Isolation

Locate your property's main water meter or main brass ball valve near the street or crawlspace entry. Turn lever 90 degrees perpendicular to the pipe.

Step 02

Bleed Trapped Pressure

Open your lowest exterior hose bib or basement wash basin faucet to bleed trapped hydraulic line pressure out of interior supply pipes.

Step 03

Isolate Electrical Breakers

If water is pooling near floor outlets or basement appliances, switch off main power at your electrical panel before stepping into wet zones.
