What To Do While Technicians Are En Route

Follow these 4 safety steps to minimize water damage while our mobile unit is dispatched.

Action 01

Shut Off The Main Water Valve

Locate your main water shutoff valve (usually near the water meter or front basement wall) and turn the gate lever 90 degrees clockwise until perpendicular to the pipe.

Action 02

Relieve Line Pressure

Open your lowest outdoor hose bib or basement faucet to bleed remaining trapped water pressure out of the plumbing system and slow down indoor leaks.

Action 03

Isolate Electrical Hazards

If water accumulation is near electrical outlets, appliances, or basement breaker panels, do not enter standing water. Shut off power at the main breaker box if safe.

Action 04

Turn Water Heater to Pilot

If main water is shut off, turn gas water heaters to "PILOT" mode or turn off electric water heater breakers to prevent heating elements from burning out dry.
