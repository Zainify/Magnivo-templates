Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know Before Booking

Clear answers to common residential and commercial plumbing questions.

How does your upfront flat-rate pricing work?
Unlike traditional plumbers who charge unpredictable hourly rates while searching for tools, HydroPro diagnostic technicians inspect the issue first and provide a binding written estimate before any work begins.
What is your emergency dispatch turnaround time?
We maintain 14 fully stocked mobile units active throughout the regional metro area 24 hours a day. Our average emergency dispatch response time is 32 minutes from the moment you confirm dispatch hotline details.
What does your 2-Year Workmanship Warranty cover?
All pipe fittings, copper solder joints, PEX connections, drain clearing services, and fixture installations performed by HydroPro come with a full 24-month parts and labor guarantee. If a leak occurs, we fix it at zero cost.
Should I choose tankless water heating or standard tank units?
Tankless water heaters deliver endless on-demand hot water, take up 80% less wall space, and consume up to 34% less energy. Our Master Plumbers will evaluate your home's gas line size or electrical panel capacity during the visit.
