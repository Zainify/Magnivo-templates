SAFETY PROTOCOLS

What to Do While Electrician is En Route

Follow these critical National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) safety guidelines while our mobile unit is traveling to your property.

1. Shut Off Main Disconnect

If safe to access without touching standing water, switch the large top 100A or 200A main toggle at your breaker panel to the OFF position.

2. Do Not Touch Sparking Outlets

Never attempt to pull a plug from an outlet that is hot to touch, discolored brown/black, or emitting visible smoke.

3. Call 911 If Active Fire

If you observe open flames or heavy smoke coming from wall cavities or the electrical service meter, evacuate and contact the Fire Dept immediately.
