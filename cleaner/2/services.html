Task Verification

Standardized Cleaning Checklists

Select a service level below to inspect room-by-room operational items.

v
Kitchen Counter Sanitization
Wipe down counter surfaces, backsplash, exterior microwave, and stainless steel appliances.
v
Bathroom Disinfection
Scrub toilet, tub, shower glass, sink basin, counter, and polish all chrome fixtures.
v
Floor Vacuuming & Mopping
HEPA-filter vacuuming of rugs/carpets and damp mopping of tile, hardwood, and laminate.
v
Accessible Surface Dusting
Dust tables, desks, nightstands, windowsills, frame tops, and entertainment centers.
v
Bed Making & Linen Tidy
Straighten bedding and make beds in all designated sleeping quarters.
v
Trash Emptying
Empty waste bins across all rooms and replace with clean liner bags.
v
Baseboard & Trim Hand Wiping
Hand-wipe baseboards, door moldings, door frames, and window sills throughout home.
v
Tile Grout & Scale Scrubbing
Scrub shower tile grout, soap scum accumulation, and lime scale on fixtures.
v
High Dusting & Vents
Dust ceiling fan blades, HVAC return vents, light fixture covers, and tall shelving.
v
Cabinet Exterior Degreasing
Hand-wipe kitchen and bathroom cabinet faces to remove smudges and grease buildup.
v
Light Switch & Outlet Wiping
Disinfect high-touch areas including door handles, switch plates, and thermostatic controls.
v
Furniture Vacuuming
Vacuum upholstered sofa cushions and under removable furniture cushions.
v
Inside Cabinet & Drawer Wiping
Vacuum and hand-wipe inside all empty kitchen, bathroom, and hallway cabinets and drawers.
v
Oven Interior Deep Cleaning
Apply heavy-duty degreaser, scrub oven walls, wire racks, and interior glass door.
v
Refrigerator Interior Cleaning
Remove shelves, wash drawers, wipe spill residue, and disinfect refrigerator walls.
v
Closet & Shelf Sanitization
Wipe down all closet shelving units, hanging rods, and corner floor recesses.
v
Inside Window Track Wiping
Clean interior window glass and vacuum dust/debris out of window tracks.
v
Full Turnover Inspection
Final quality control walkthrough ensuring property meets landlord security deposit standards.
